<h2>Exhibition statement</h2>
<p><em>Philosophical Abstractions</em> used early generative image systems to investigate logic, fallacies, rules, reason, and other concepts that exist primarily through thought rather than direct observation.</p>
<p>The project asked VQGAN and CLIP-guided systems to interpret abstract philosophical language. The resulting images were records of a machine attempting to translate conceptual structures into visual form.</p>
<p>Many works began with photographs from Davila's personal life and were transformed through image-to-image processes, preserving private layers of memory beneath public philosophical titles.</p>
