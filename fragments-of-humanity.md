<h2>Project statement</h2>
<p><em>Fragments of Humanity</em> begins from a difficult fact: human beings are routinely judged before they are understood. Physical appearance influences who is perceived as trustworthy, vulnerable, desirable, threatening, intelligent, or fully human.</p>
<p>The series fractures and reconstructs recognizable bodies without eliminating their humanity, interrupting ordinary habits of recognition and judgment.</p>
