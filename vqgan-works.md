<h2>Historical context</h2>
<p>Most works in this group began with photographs, drawings, or visual studies transformed through VQGAN and CLIP-guided systems. The process was slow, unstable, and materially different from later prompt-based image generators.</p>
<p>The blockchain links associated with these works belong to the earlier N1FTEY identity and are best understood as provenance records rather than the primary frame for the current practice.</p>
